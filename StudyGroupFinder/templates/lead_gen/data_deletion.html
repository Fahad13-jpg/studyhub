Request deletion from Lead-Gen-system

Effective date: 6 November 2026

Deletion requests are handled manually. This public page does not look up or delete records automatically, and no login is required to read these instructions.

  1. Email mohmmadfahad53408@gmail.com with the subject Lead-Gen-system data deletion request.
  2. Provide the organization/business name and the email address or WhatsApp phone number involved. If applicable, describe the approximate date of a message or the connected account. Share only what is necessary to locate your records.
  3. Say whether you want a communication record deleted, an integration disconnected, or a broader account/organization deletion. Do not include passwords, access tokens, app secrets or unnecessary identity documents.

What happens next

We will review the request, verify authority where necessary, identify relevant records and explain the action taken or any further information needed. A requested deletion is not complete merely because the email was sent; ask for confirmation or a status update if you have not received one.

Depending on the request, relevant lead/contact information, campaign research, communication content and connected-credential records can be reviewed for removal or disconnection. If an organization controls the data, we may need its authorized administrator's involvement. We will explain any records that cannot immediately be removed, including necessary suppression records, operational/security records, backups or applicable legal obligations.

Provider accounts and opt-outs

Deleting information from Lead-Gen-system does not itself delete messages from a recipient's phone or information independently held by Meta, Google or another provider. Those copies may require a separate provider request. Removing an app's authorization at the provider stops future access but does not automatically erase previously stored project data; contact us for that review.

To stop WhatsApp campaign contact, reply STOP where the channel is active or email the contact above. We may retain the minimum suppression information needed to honor that request. A general incoming-message inbox and automatic deletion dashboard are not currently offered.

Read the privacy policy for the data categories and purposes covered by this project.
